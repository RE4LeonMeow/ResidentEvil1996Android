Resident Evil PC Decompilation Android launcher prototype 1.00

APK icon: supplied by the project maintainer as an ICO file. The largest
256x256 image is retained without resampling; Android supplies the icon mask.
Resident Evil artwork is separately owned and is not relicensed by GPL.
Based on https://github.com/ecruells/resident-evil-pc-decomp
Copyright and licensing: see RE1-GPL-3.0.txt and the source distribution.
No gameplay data is included. Supply your own compatible GOG/1997 USA data.
Launcher artwork is composited from images supplied by the user. Underlying
Resident Evil artwork and trademarks remain with their respective rightsholders;
the artwork is separate from the GPL-licensed application code.

https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg - LGPL 2.1 or later, no GPL/nonfree flags.
zlib 1.2.13 - https://zlib.net/ - zlib license; see zlib.txt. Used for local HD pack decompression.
SDL 2.30.8 - https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL - zlib license.
Emscripten 3.1.69 - https://github.com/emscripten-core/emscripten - MIT/University of Illinois NCSA licenses.
The source distribution includes dependency source archives and build instructions.
